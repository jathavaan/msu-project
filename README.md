# Financial Helper - Group BouvetOne

## Functionality
**Add recurring income and expenses with due dates**
- Subscriptions (overview with all the subscriptions)
- Bus tickets
- Rent/House loan down payment
- Student loan down payment
- Car
- Money for fun
- Food
- Electricity

**Budget**
- Set budgets for different posts (i.e. food and drinks)
- Categories

**Upcoming payments**
- Next 7 days
- Upcoming bills with dates

**Saving**
- Saving goals
- How much you save or use of your savings each month

**Graph with how much money you have on your account**
- Use the monthly income and expenses with dates to display a graph

**Store discount codes**
- Store images of discount codes, or just the dicsount code, store and when it expires
- Remind users that a coupon is about to expire

**AI agent**
- Talk about your economics and analyze your expenses. Ask for advice and identify things that drains money
- AI agent with recommendations on where to put the money (i.e. funds, intrests)
